## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is the Rawdah: white marble arches with gilded capitals standing on a royal-green garden carpet, where gold vines grow along every arch. It refuses the green-and-gold card portal with blurred blobs, gradient headings and floating rounded cards.

OWN-WORLD: White marble ground (#FAFAF7, faint silver veining) for reading; royal green carpet field (#0B4A37, deep #073326) with a woven gold floral pattern owns the hero, page bands and footer; silver (#9AA5A8, light #D7DDDF) for hairlines, rules and quiet type; gold (#B8893A, leaf #D4AF5A) for capitals, vine illumination and the one primary action. Amiri for headings like Naskh inscriptions, Noto Kufi for UI, Hafs for verses. Components are arches: rounded mihrab tops, gold hairline inner frame, silver column rules.

STORY: The visitor sees the verse of the day framed in a white marble arch on the green carpet, knows this is a Quran platform, searches or taps an arched section, and returns daily.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Green carpet field; a tall white marble arch centred, flanked by two slender columns with gold capitals; gold vines draw themselves up both sides of the arch and leaves unfurl; the verse of the day in large Hafs inside the arch with its surah reference; search field at the arch base as the primary action with "متابعة القراءة" and "استمع"; the first arched section row peeks below on white marble.
