# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Everyone: young visitors arriving from the oxeta.ai Instagram channel on their phones, Muslims of all ages using it daily for reading, listening, adhkar and prayer times, and students of knowledge looking for tafsir, tajweed and Quranic sciences. Arabic first, right to left; mobile is the main device.

## Product Purpose

منصة القرآن الكريم: one place to read and listen to the Quran, follow tafsir, learn tajweed and the Quranic sciences, read Sahih hadith (Nabawi and Qudsi), the Names of Allah, adhkar, prayer times and qibla, and the stories of the 25 prophets with verse-by-verse cinematic films. Success: a visitor finds what they came for quickly and stays to read, listen or watch.

## Positioning

Verified content presented with care: every verse in Uthmani script from the Mushaf text, every hadith from Sahih al-Bukhari or Muslim with its number, and prophet stories told verse by verse with films drawn in code that never depict people.

## Operating Context

Visitors come from Instagram reels and stories (link in bio), from search, and as returning daily users. Sessions are often short and on a phone; reading and listening sessions can be long. Signed-in users keep bookmarks, notes and settings in a dashboard.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Sections: home, surahs and surah reader, reciters, tafsir, tajweed, Quranic sciences, hadith Nabawi, hadith Qudsi, Asma Allah al-Husna, adhkar, prayer times, qibla, prophets (index, story pages with films), bookmarks, dashboard (notes, settings), about, auth.
- Stack: Next.js 14 app router, Tailwind, framer-motion, deployed on Vercel. Content generated by verified build scripts; must stay byte-exact.
- Quran text uses the KFGQPC Hafs font (`font-hafs`).
- All sections are equally important; no single section dominates the home page.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: منصة القرآن الكريم.
- No emoji anywhere on the site.
- No depiction of prophets, companions or any people; prophets appear only as light.
- Only verified content: never paraphrase a verse as a quotation, never add unsourced hadith or claims.
- No music.

## Evidence on Hand

Real content in the repo: Quran text and translation (quran-json), hadith collections, prophet stories spec (content/prophet-stories.spec.json), reciter data. No testimonials, user counts or press exist; none may be invented.

## Product Principles

1. The text of the Quran is the hero; nothing decorates over it.
2. Trust is earned by sources shown, not by claims.
3. Every section is one tap away on a phone.
4. Reverence over spectacle: motion and color serve calm and focus.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Arabic RTL throughout, readable Quran text sizes, works on low-end phones and slow connections, respects reduced motion.
